How it works

  • 25 statements, five to a page. For each one, say how well it describes you.
  • There is no right answer and no midpoint: pick the closest fit.
  • Pressing Next locks that page for good. There is no back button, so check your five before you move on.
  • On the last page the button says Submit, and pressing it finishes the survey.
  • Every click is saved. If you close the browser or lose connection, you carry on from the first unlocked page.
  • It takes about ten minutes.
