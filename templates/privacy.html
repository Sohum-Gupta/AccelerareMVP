Privacy

This page says, in plain words, what Accelerare keeps about you, who can see it, and how to have it deleted.

What we keep

  • Your name, your email addresses and your phone number, as you gave them.
  • Your password, in a scrambled form that nobody can read, our staff included.
  • Your answers to the survey, and whether you said you had taken it before.
  • Which organizations you belong to on Accelerare, and the licences you hold.
  • If you buy a licence: the amount, the date and a reference from Stripe, our payment company. Your card details go to Stripe and never reach us.

We also keep counts, such as how many people signed up in a week. These counts are not linked to you.

Who can see it

You see your account details and, for each survey, its dates and results level. After you submit a survey you see its results, not your answers.

An organization you take the survey for. Its admins can see your name, the email address it knows you by, whether your survey is in progress or submitted, the dates, its results level, and a mark if it looks as though you have taken the survey before. They never see:

  • your answers;
  • your phone number;
  • any survey you take personally, or for another organization.

Accelerare staff. Support staff can see your account and your licences, so that they can help you. They cannot read your answers. A small number of administrators can.

If two accounts look as though they belong to the same person, an administrator may compare them and link them. Nothing is linked automatically.

Companies that work for us

  • Amazon Web Services runs our servers and database, in the United States, and sends our emails.
  • Stripe takes payments and sends the receipt.
  • Sentry receives a report when something goes wrong on the site. A report does not contain your name, your email address or your answers.
  • Better Stack checks that the site is up. It sees nothing about you.

We do not sell your information. Nobody else receives it, apart from what this page describes.

Cookies and email

The site uses only the cookies it needs: one that keeps you logged in, one that keeps forms safe, and one that carries a short message to the next page. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

We email you to confirm an email address, to reset your password, when an organization invites you, and when you are given a licence. We do not send marketing email.

Deleting your account

You can ask for your account to be deleted. {% if contact_email %}Write to {{ contact_email }} from the email address you log in with, so that we know it is you.{% else %}Ask the person at Accelerare who gave you access, or the admin of your organization, who can put you in touch. We act on your request once it comes from the email address you log in with, so that we know it is you.{% endif %}

An admin of an organization you belong to can also ask us to delete your account.

  • If you never finished a survey, everything about you is deleted.
  • If you finished one, we remove your name, email addresses, phone number and login, and keep the survey with nothing that links it to you. An organization you took it for then sees "Erased member" in place of your name.
  • Copies in our backups are gone within 30 days.

Two things are outside our reach: a list that an organization's admin has already downloaded (it holds names and email addresses, never answers), and Stripe's own record of a payment.
