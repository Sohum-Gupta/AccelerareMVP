This sends two errors on purpose, so that you can see them arrive in Sentry: one from the web site and one from the background worker.

  • When you press the button you will see the site's "Something went wrong" page. That is the test working, not a fault. Use your browser's Back button to return here.
  • A job called "test_error" appears in the list. It fails once, on purpose, and is tried again about two minutes later, when it ends as "Done". Nothing is left looking broken.
  • In Sentry, look for two new errors named "DeliberateError". The one from the web site shows your account's number and no name or email address.
  • If Sentry is set to email you about new errors, you will get an email. Nothing else is affected.
