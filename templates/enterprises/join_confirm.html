You have not joined yet. First, here is what the admins of {{ organization_name }} will be able to see if you do.

About you

As soon as you join, its admins can see:

  • your name{% if name %}: {{ name }}{% else %} (you have not added one yet; you can on your profile){% endif %};
  • your email address: {{ email }} {% if by_invite %}(the address the invite was sent to){% else %}(the address you log in with){% endif %};
  • the date you joined, and whether you are a member, an admin or no longer active;
  • whether you have a licence to take the survey for {{ organization_name }}, and its level.

If you take the survey for {{ organization_name }}

Its admins can also see:

  • that you are taking the survey for them, and whether it is in progress or submitted;
  • the dates you started and submitted it;
  • its results level, and a mark if it looks as though you have taken the survey before;
  • your results for that survey, once results are available.

What they never see

  • your phone number;
  • your answers to the survey;
  • how far through a survey you are;
  • any survey you take personally, or for another organization.

Members who are not admins see nothing about you.

Joining makes you a member. To take the survey for {{ organization_name }} you also need a licence from it. It may give you one as you join, or later.
